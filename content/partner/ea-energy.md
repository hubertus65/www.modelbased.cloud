[EA Systems Dresden GmbH](https://www.ea-energie.de/en/) (EASD) is a Dresden-based engineering company
specializing in model-based design, simulation and monitoring of complex energy systems.
Founded in 2006, EASD has built deep expertise at the intersection of physical simulation,
digital twins, and practical energy system engineering — serving municipalities, utilities
and industrial customers across Europe.

EASD is the developer of the [Blue Energy Library](/libraries/blueenergy/),
a Modelica simulation library for distributed energy systems running on
[Modelon Impact](/tools/modelica/). The library covers heating, cooling,
power generation, storage, e-mobility and hydrogen — backed by more than
15 years of hands-on project experience.
Beyond the Blue Energy Library, EASD's product and service portfolio spans
three tightly integrated practice areas: consulting for energy system design
and model-based optimization, a suite of simulation and planning tools including 
district heating grid simulation,
and technical monitoring solutions including scientific energy monitoring and
real-time digital twin platforms.

MBI collaborates with EASD on reselling and deploying the Blue Energy Library
within the Modelon Impact ecosystem, and on consulting projects that combine
EASD's deep energy-domain knowledge with MBI's expertise in model-based
design processes and Modelon Impact.
