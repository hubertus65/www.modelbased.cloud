## Services

The Team from MBI has over 20 years of experience in modeling and simulation, and the development of model-based workflows and processes for a wide range of industries. We can support you with a whole spectrum of services — from designing your processes, teams, and workflows, to figuring out the right abstraction for your models by applying the right math.

We offer [Consulting](/services/consulting/) across Model Based Design, Modelica development, and FMI integration, as well as [Training](/services/training/) for Modelica, open standards, and Modelon Impact.
